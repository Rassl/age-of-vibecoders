Privacy

Short version: there are no accounts and the games do not ask for or store personal data. Here is everything the pages do talk to.

Accounts and data

You never sign in. The games keep small preferences (for example the last city you chose in Ascent, or which console panels you folded away) in your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Analytics

The Squad Runner page loads Yandex Metrica, a web analytics service, to count visits and see which parts of the game people reach. It sets cookies and records page views, approximate location derived from your IP address, and device and browser type. See Yandex's privacy policy. The hub and Ascent pages do not load analytics.

Third-party requests in Ascent

To draw the world, Ascent fetches data directly from public services; those services see your IP address in the normal course of serving a request:

  • satellite imagery tiles from Esri (ArcGIS World Imagery);
  • building outlines from OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API;
  • landmark names and summaries from Wikipedia and Wikidata.

Location

Ascent can start from where you are. That happens only if you choose My location, after which the browser asks for your permission. The coordinates are used to centre the flight and are not stored or sent to us.

Fonts and hosting

The hub pages load fonts from Google Fonts. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Neither we nor the games sell or share data with anyone.
